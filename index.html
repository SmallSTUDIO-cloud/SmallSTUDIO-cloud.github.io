Find a Business Name That Actually Works

Brand//Grade is a free business name analyzer, brand name checker, and name generator designed to help you evaluate and improve names for startups, companies, products, shops, apps, agencies, creators, and new ventures.

Business Name Analyzer

Enter a name and see how it performs across memorability, brand strength, clarity, uniqueness, and pronunciation.

Business Name Generator

Generate name ideas from your business type and preferred naming direction, then analyze the names you like.

Brand Name Checker

Use the score and feedback to identify strengths, weaknesses, and areas that may need refinement before choosing a name.

How the Brand//Grade Score Works

Memorability

Looks at characteristics that can make a name easier to recognize and recall.

Brand Strength

Looks at whether the name has characteristics that can support a distinctive brand identity.

Clarity

Evaluates whether the wording is understandable without unnecessary confusion.

Uniqueness

Looks for characteristics that can make a name feel distinctive rather than generic.

Pronunciation

Evaluates how straightforward the name is to read, say, and communicate.

Important: Brand//Grade provides an informational naming assessment. A score does not guarantee commercial success or confirm trademark, company-registration, domain, or social-media availability. Perform separate legal and availability checks before adopting a name.

Business Name Analyzer FAQ

What is a business name analyzer?

A business name analyzer evaluates practical characteristics of a potential business or brand name and provides feedback about its strengths and weaknesses.

How does Brand//Grade rate a business name?

Brand//Grade evaluates a name across memorability, brand strength, clarity, uniqueness, and pronunciation, then combines those dimensions into an overall informational score.

Can I analyze any business name?

Yes. You can analyze names for businesses, startups, products, shops, services, apps, agencies, projects, and other brand concepts.

Is Brand//Grade free?

The core Brand//Grade name analysis and generation features are available for free in the browser.

Does a high score mean a name is legally available?

No. A Brand//Grade score does not confirm trademark availability, company registration availability, domain availability, or social-media username availability. Those checks require separate research.

Can Brand//Grade generate business name ideas?

Yes. Brand//Grade includes a name-generation tool that helps users explore potential business and brand names.
